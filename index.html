GET TO KNOW ME

About Me

I’m a senior at Iowa State University studying Event Management and Marketing with a minor in Entrepreneurship. I’m passionate about creating meaningful experiences through thoughtful event planning, creative design, and strategic marketing.

I especially enjoy weddings and social events because they combine creativity, organization, communication, and attention to detail. I love seeing an event go from an idea to something people can experience and remember.

COLLEGE EXPERIENCE

Life at Iowa State

My time at Iowa State has given me opportunities to grow both academically and professionally. Through coursework, campus involvement, internships, and event experiences, I’ve developed skills in organization, communication, leadership, marketing, and event operations.

Being involved on campus has also allowed me to work with different people, take on new responsibilities, and gain hands-on experience outside of the classroom.

PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE

Work Experience

My work experience has allowed me to explore weddings, university events, event operations, marketing, and guest experiences. I’ve gained experience with event setup, timelines, vendor communication, client interaction, social media, promotion, and day-of coordination.

These experiences have strengthened my interest in building a career in the wedding and event industry while continuing to develop my skills in marketing and creative strategy.

LOOKING AHEAD

My Goals

My goal is to continue building experience in the event industry, especially within weddings and social events. I hope to combine my background in event management, marketing, and entrepreneurship to eventually create a business of my own.
