Hi, I'm Lizzie!

I’m a senior at Iowa State University studying Event Management and Marketing with a minor in Entrepreneurship. I’m passionate about creating meaningful experiences through thoughtful event planning, creative design, and strategic marketing, with a special interest in weddings and social events.

I enjoy combining creativity with organization to transform ideas into experiences people remember. Whether I am helping coordinate an event, developing marketing content, or working behind the scenes, I enjoy seeing all of the small details come together to create something meaningful.

Life at Iowa State

My time at Iowa State University has given me opportunities to explore both the creative and business sides of the event industry. Through my Event Management and Marketing coursework, campus involvement, internships, and professional experiences, I have developed skills in communication, organization, marketing, leadership, and event operations.

Being involved on campus has also allowed me to gain hands-on experience outside of the classroom. These opportunities have helped me better understand how events are planned, promoted, and executed while connecting me with professionals and other students who share similar interests.

Work Experience

My professional experiences have allowed me to work in several areas of the event industry, including weddings, university events, event operations, and marketing. Through these positions, I have gained experience with event setup, timelines, vendor communication, client interaction, social media, promotion, and day-of event coordination.

My wedding and event experience has especially strengthened my interest in creating thoughtful and memorable celebrations. I enjoy the combination of creativity, organization, teamwork, and problem-solving that goes into successfully bringing an event from an idea to reality.
